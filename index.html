A Lightweight Temporal Model for Heart Rate Estimation from Wrist PPG under Daily-Life Motion

Chinh Tan Ly, Tuan Kiet Pham, Duy Trung Do, Phi Phong Pham and Quang Linh Huynh. Submitted; under revision, decision pending.

A temporal deep learning pipeline for estimating heart rate from wrist PPG and accelerometer signals affected by motion artifacts.

  • Synchronized 8-second PPG/ACC windows, accelerometer resampling, signal normalization and artifact-aware preprocessing.
  • Temporal modeling with a residual encoder, spectral/context features and a regime-conditioned mixture of experts.
  • Strict 15-fold leave-one-subject-out evaluation over 64,697 windows, activity-level analysis, six controlled ablation contrasts and TorchScript export.

4.307 ± 2.020 bpmRaw macro MAE across 15 held-out subjects. Decoded: 4.278 ± 1.935 bpm; complete pipeline: 4.491 ± 1.896 bpm.

Residual CNN and temporal attention were supported by the controlled ablations. The complete post-processing pipeline did not improve mean error; no on-device validation is claimed.

Human Behavior Recognition Using 2D Time-Series Data and Machine Learning Models

Chinh Tan Ly and Quang Linh Huynh. Journal of Physics: Conference Series, 3180, 012004 (2026).

Eight-class activity recognition from 2D pose time series. The accompanying repository extends the original work with a reproducible evaluation and inference pipeline.

  • Torso-relative normalization of 17 COCO keypoints, 150-frame windows, and TSFEL plus handcrafted motion and geometry features.
  • HistGradientBoosting classification, training-only feature selection and nested subject-level model selection.
  • Post-paper development: nested Viterbi decoding, unseen-participant inference and five-subject LOSO evaluation, reporting 73.31% accuracy and 76.39% macro F1.

The repository's later LOSO results use a different protocol from the paper's original window-level evaluation.

Internship project · ECG domain adaptation

Adapting an ECG classifier to AD8232 recordings

Evaluated a MIT-BIH/SVDB-trained classifier on a 30-minute, single-lead AD8232 recording, investigated ventricular ectopic beat failures and adapted its temporal classification stage.

  • Audited channel representation, calibration and temporal-context mismatch.
  • Retrained the Stage-B bidirectional GRU with attention while keeping Stage A and the final calibration rules fixed.
  • Split the raw recording before feature extraction and prevented temporal context from crossing evaluation boundaries.

Macro F1: 0.513 → 0.967Frozen vs. adapted model · 2,062 eligible beats · five-fold temporal out-of-fold evaluation

Results describe adaptation within one recording. Some abnormal beats may be synthetic; independent-session validation is pending.
